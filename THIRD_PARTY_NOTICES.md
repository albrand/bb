## Conductor theme for bb

The built-in palettes "Conductor" and "Conductor Pitch Black"
(`apps/app/src/lib/themes/conductor.ts`, `conductor-black.ts`) are adapted
from bb-plugin-conductor-theme by bottlebrushes, commit f640f51,
https://github.com/bottlebrushes/bb-plugin-conductor-theme. The remote font
imports were replaced with vendored fonts and unused rules were removed.
